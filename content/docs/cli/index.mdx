---
title: CLI
description: Scaffolding projects and resources, and the watching development loop.
---

`ulo-cli` does three things: creates a project that compiles, generates a wired module/controller/service triple, and runs a watch loop that rebuilds and restarts on save. The dev loop can also hold the listening socket across restarts, so a rebuild does not refuse connections.
